# Zenlytic LLM Data Use and Retention Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Zenlytic is a product of Chord Commerce, Inc. In this policy, "Zenlytic," "we" and "us" refer to Chord Commerce, Inc. as the provider of the Zenlytic service. Zenlytic uses third-party large language model (LLM) providers to power Zoë and other AI features. This policy explains how customer data sent to those providers is used and how long it is kept.

## No training on customer data

Zenlytic does not use Customer Content to train AI models. Under the commercial terms that govern our use of each provider's API, providers do not use customer prompts or outputs to train their models.

## Provider retention

* OpenAI: prompts and outputs may be retained in OpenAI's abuse monitoring logs for up to 30 days
* Anthropic (Claude), via Google Cloud Vertex AI: prompts and outputs may be retained for up to 30 days
* Google (Gemini), via Google Cloud Vertex AI: prompts and outputs may be retained for up to 30 days

Retained data is used only to detect misuse and is not used to train models.

## Customer-controlled routing

Customers can route LLM traffic to their own LLM instance. In that case, the customer's own agreement with that provider governs retention and use.

## Changes

We will notify customers of changes to LLM providers or retention periods as set out in the Data Processing Addendum and AI Addendum.
